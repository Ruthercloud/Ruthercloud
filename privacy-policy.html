Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how RutherCloud collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Introduction

RutherCloud Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("RutherCloud", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal data when you visit our website, contact us, submit an inquiry or use our services.

We aim to handle personal data in accordance with applicable Indian data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the applicable DPDP Rules, 2025, as and when their respective provisions come into force.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: Name, email address and phone number.
  • Business details: Company or organisation name and business-related information.
  • Inquiry details: Project requirements, messages, service interests and information you choose to submit.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, website activity and server logs, where collected.
  • Communication records: Emails, inquiry history and other communications with our team.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our general website contact form. Please avoid submitting unnecessary personal or confidential information.

3. How We Collect Personal Data

We may collect information when you:

  • Submit a contact or inquiry form on our website.
  • Contact us through email, telephone or WhatsApp.
  • Request a consultation, quotation or business proposal.
  • Interact with our website and its technical features.
  • Communicate with us during a business relationship.

4. Purpose of Processing

We use personal data only for relevant business purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries and contacting you about your request.
  • Understanding project requirements and preparing quotations or proposals.
  • Providing, managing and supporting our services.
  • Communicating project updates and business-related information.
  • Maintaining website functionality, security and performance.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Handling complaints, disputes and requests relating to personal data.

We will not use personal data for an unrelated purpose without an applicable lawful basis and, where required, providing notice and obtaining consent.

5. Consent and Your Choices

Where consent is required under applicable law, we will provide a clear notice explaining the personal data involved and the specific purpose of processing before obtaining consent.

You may withdraw consent through the available communication method or by contacting us at info@ruthercloud.com. Withdrawal will not affect processing that was lawfully carried out before the withdrawal.

We will explain any consequences of withdrawal where continued processing is necessary to provide a requested service or meet a legal obligation.

6. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell personal data. We may share relevant information only when reasonably necessary with:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email, communication and customer-support providers.
  • Analytics or website performance providers, if enabled.
  • Professional advisers and service providers assisting our operations.
  • Government authorities or law enforcement where legally required.

Where third-party service providers process personal data on our behalf, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that such processing is governed by applicable contractual and legal safeguards.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, performance measurement and analytics.

Where non-essential cookies or analytics require consent under applicable law, we will provide an appropriate notice and consent mechanism.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, provide requested services, maintain business records or comply with applicable legal obligations.

When personal data is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Where applicable, statutory retention periods and prescribed requirements under the DPDP Rules will be followed.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

Depending on the systems involved, these measures may include:

  • Access controls and restricted data access.
  • Secure hosting and system configuration.
  • Appropriate encryption or other data protection measures.
  • Backups, monitoring and security reviews.
  • Appropriate safeguards in service-provider arrangements.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Personal Data Breach

If a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps to investigate, contain and mitigate its impact.

Where required by applicable law, we will notify affected individuals and the relevant authority within the prescribed manner and timelines.

11. International Data Transfers

Some of our hosting, communication or technology service providers may process information using infrastructure located outside India.

Where personal data is transferred outside India, we will follow applicable Indian laws and any restrictions or requirements notified by the Central Government.

12. Children's Personal Data

Our website and business services are intended for business users and are not directed towards children under 18 years of age.

We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal data through our general website forms. Where processing of a child's personal data is permitted and undertaken, we will follow the applicable parental consent, verification and other legal requirements.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and its commencement, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about personal data being processed.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure of personal data where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for consent-based processing.
  • Raise a grievance regarding the handling of your personal data.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights where permitted by law.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us at info@ruthercloud.com. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

These rights are subject to applicable legal requirements, exceptions and commencement dates.

14. Grievance Redressal

If you have a concern, complaint or request relating to your personal data or this Privacy Policy, you may contact our privacy contact using the details below.

We will acknowledge and address grievances within a reasonable period and in accordance with the timelines prescribed under applicable law.

Under the applicable DPDP Rules, the grievance response period is required to be published and cannot exceed 90 days once the relevant provision comes into force.

15. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal data.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business practices, technology or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
